C128 preview — VDC 80 columns: 640×200 bitmap, 2 of 16 RGBI colours per 8×8 cell, RGBI monitor shape (click to paint · arrow keys move camera)

Tiles are converted from the C64 set automatically: each 8×8 cell gets the foreground/background pair that fits best, and ordered dithering stands in for its other colours. Hero and monsters are drawn into the cells (soft sprites); a cell with body pixels takes the figure's colour as its foreground. The panel on the right shows the map name and position.

VIC-20: each cell has one colour of its own (pen 10, colours 0–7). Screen, border and auxiliary colours are shared by the whole screen and set per map in the Map tab.
C128: each 8×8 cell has a foreground (pen 1) and a background colour (pen 0), any of the 16 RGBI colours.
A partly transparent cell either writes its colors (pixels underneath get recolored) or keeps the colors underneath (its own pixels get recolored). The map preview shows the result.
